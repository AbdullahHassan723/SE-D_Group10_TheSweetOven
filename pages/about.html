Our Home Kitchen Story

Meet the Baker Behind The Sweet Oven

Founded in 2021 by home pastry chef Hira Salman in Street 14, Sector F-8/3, Islamabad, The Sweet Oven began with one convection oven, handwritten family recipes, and a promise never to cut corners with shortening or artificial essence.

What started as weekend fudge cakes and cinnamon rolls for neighbours now serves hundreds of families across Islamabad and Rawalpindi — still baked in small, numbered daily batches.
