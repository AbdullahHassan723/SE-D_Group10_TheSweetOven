Meet the baker.

Sector F-8/3 • Home Kitchen Since 2021

Founded in 2021 by home pastry chef Hira Salman in Street 14, Sector F-8/3, Islamabad, The Sweet Oven began with one convection oven, handwritten family recipes, and a promise never to cut corners with shortening or artificial essence.

Every sponge, brownie tray, and Lotus tres leches tub is mixed by hand in small daily batches using real cultured butter and 54% Belgian dark cocoa — serving hundreds of families across Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
